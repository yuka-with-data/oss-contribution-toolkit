# Future of Open Source Contribution
Open source contribution continues to evolve alongside advances in AI, developer tools, and software engineering practices.
This document outlines the direction this repository aims to follow as these workflows continue to change.

## AI Is Changing Open Source
AI is making it easier to understand unfamiliar codebases, draft documentation, explain concepts, and general implementation ideas. As a result, contributors can spend less time on repetitive tasks and more time solving meaningful problems.

## From AI Assistant to AI Collaboration
Today's AI tools primarily assist developers with coding and writing. Emerging workflows are moving toward AI systems that can help plan tasks, analyze repositories, review changes, and collaborate throughout the contribution process.
