# Workflow Principles
Most open source projects follow a similar workflow, even if the tools and process differ.

The purpose of the workflow is NOT to help contributors and maintainers collaborate efficiently while keeping projects stable and maintainable.

## Start with Understanding
Before making changes, understand the problem you are trying to address and solve.

This often means:
- Reading the issue carefully
- Reviewing relevant documentation
- Understanding project goald and constraints

A well-understood problem usually leads to a better solution.

## Align Before You Build
Not every idea should immediately become a pull request.

For larger changes, contributors are often encouraged to discuss their approach first through issues, discussions or other project communication channels.

Early alignment can prevent duplicated work and reduce unnecessary revisions later.

## Prefer Small, Focused Changes
Smaller contributions are easier to review, test, and maintain.

When possible:
- Solve one problem at a time
- Keep changes focused
- Avoid unrelated modifications

A series of small improvement is often more effective than a single large contribution.
