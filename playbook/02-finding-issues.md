# Finding Issues
Most first contribution start with choosing the right issue.

Good issue selection matters more than coding ability. A well-scoped issue increases your change of success and reduces frustration.

## What to Look For
Begin with issues that are:
- Labeled as `good first issue` or `beginner-friendly`
- Small in scope and clearly defined
- Focused on documentation, UI text, or minor bugs
- Already discussed or confirmed by maintainers

Avoid starting with:
- Large feature requests
- Unclear or vague issues
- Highly technical refactors (unless guided)
